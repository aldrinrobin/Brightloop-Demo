# ✨ Brightloop — Subscription Management Demo

> A smarter way to manage subscriptions, customers, and business operations.

**Brightloop** is a demo implementation of a role-based subscription management platform designed to simplify subscription operations for businesses. It aims to bring administrators, sales representatives, and customers together through a centralized platform with dedicated dashboards and streamlined workflows.

## 🎯 Project Overview

Managing subscriptions, customer information, and sales operations can become complicated when handled across multiple systems.

Brightloop explores a centralized solution where different users can access the tools and information relevant to their roles, making subscription management more organized and efficient.

This project is being developed as a demonstration of the proposed platform and its core workflows.

## 👥 User Roles

Brightloop is designed around three primary user roles:

### 1. Administrator
- Manage sales representatives and customer accounts.
- Monitor subscription operations.
- Access an overview of business activities.
- Manage platform-level information.

### 2. Sales Representative
- Access an individual dashboard.
- Manage assigned customers.
- Track customer subscriptions.
- Monitor sales-related activities.

### 3. Customer
- Access a personalized dashboard.
- View subscription information.
- Manage personal account details.
- Track subscription status.

## 🚀 Key Features

The demo aims to showcase:

- **Role-Based Dashboards:** Separate interfaces for administrators, sales representatives, and customers.
- **Centralized Management:** A unified approach to managing customer and subscription information.
- **User Authentication:** Role-aware access to the platform.
- **Customer Management:** Organized customer information and account management.
- **Subscription Tracking:** An overview of subscription details and their status.
- **Responsive Interface:** An accessible and intuitive user experience.

*Features are being developed progressively. Not all listed functionality is currently implemented.*
